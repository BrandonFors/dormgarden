# DormGarden

Dorms often aren't suitable places for many house plants. Dorms often lack significant lighting or space to place a houseplant by a window. Students are often busy and forget to properly monitor plants that require more attention. Students might leave for breaks and not be able to find someone able to water a more demanding plant for them.

This project aims to provide a minimally invasive solution that enables you to grow more demanding houseplants within your dorm. 

It will monitor soil moisture, temperature, and lighting. You will be able to configure when DormGarden should water your plant and when the grow lights should turn on/off. You can configure it to water your plant on a set schedule or detect when you've forotten to water the plant so it can pick up your slack. Overall, it offers you a minimal solution to keeping many houseplants healthy within your dorm when conflicts arise from college life.
